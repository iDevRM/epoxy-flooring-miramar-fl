Polyaspartic Floor Coatings in Miramar, FL

The most Florida-friendly floor coating available — UV-stable, fast-curing, and ready for vehicles and foot traffic the same day as installation.

Why Polyaspartic Is Built for Florida

Standard epoxy has one major vulnerability in South Florida: UV exposure. Aromatic epoxy topcoats turn yellow or amber when exposed to direct sunlight — a serious problem for Miramar garages and any space with natural light. Polyaspartic is an aliphatic coating that is inherently UV-stable — it won't yellow, ever.

On top of that, polyaspartic cures in 1–4 hours (vs. 12–24 for epoxy), so we can complete a full system — primer, base coat, flake broadcast, and topcoat — in a single day. Cars return to the garage the same evening.

Polyaspartic FAQ — Miramar, FL

How much does polyaspartic coating cost in Miramar? +

Polyaspartic systems run $4–$8 per square foot installed — slightly more than standard epoxy due to material cost. A 2-car garage typically runs $2,500–$4,500. The premium is offset by one-day installation and superior UV performance in South Florida's climate. Call (954) 495-8331 for a free estimate.

Is polyaspartic just a topcoat, or can it be the full system? +

Both. We frequently use polyaspartic as the topcoat over an epoxy base coat (a hybrid system) — this gives you the chemical resistance of epoxy with the UV stability of polyaspartic. We also install full polyaspartic systems (primer + base + topcoat, all polyaspartic) for maximum performance and fastest cure times.

Can polyaspartic be installed in Florida's summer heat? +

Yes — polyaspartic has a much wider temperature installation range than standard epoxy. We can apply it in summer temps without the pot life and bubbling issues that affect epoxy in high heat. However, prep work (grinding and patching) must still be done in reasonable humidity conditions.

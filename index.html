Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from Miramar homeowners and business owners about epoxy flooring.

How long does epoxy flooring last in Florida's climate? +

When properly installed with diamond-grinding surface prep, a quality epoxy floor lasts 15–20 years in South Florida. The key is using UV-stable topcoats (or polyaspartic) to prevent yellowing from Miramar's intense sun, and ensuring the concrete is fully cured and free of moisture vapor before coating.

Can epoxy be applied over existing cracks in my garage floor? +

Yes — but the cracks must be repaired first. We fill hairline cracks with epoxy crack filler and route/fill larger cracks before applying any coating. If cracks indicate structural movement or active moisture intrusion, we'll address that before proceeding. Never apply epoxy over untreated cracks; it will telegraph through within months.

How long do I have to stay off the floor after epoxy installation? +

Light foot traffic is typically safe after 24 hours. We recommend waiting 48–72 hours before moving vehicles back in for full cure. In Miramar's summer heat, curing can actually speed up slightly. We'll give you a specific timeline based on the coating system installed and current weather conditions.

Is epoxy flooring slippery when wet? +

Standard epoxy can be slippery when wet. We always add non-slip aggregate (aluminum oxide or sand broadcast) to garage floors and commercial spaces — particularly important in Miramar where afternoon thunderstorms bring tracked-in water. Decorative flake systems also naturally add texture and grip.

What's the difference between epoxy and polyaspartic coating? +

Epoxy is a two-part coating (resin + hardener) that cures slowly (12–24 hours) and provides excellent chemical resistance. Polyaspartic cures in 1–4 hours, is UV-stable from day one (won't yellow in the Florida sun), and can be installed in one day. Polyaspartic costs more but is ideal for garage floors and outdoor-adjacent spaces in Miramar's sunny climate.

Do you offer free estimates for epoxy flooring in Miramar? +

Yes — all estimates are completely free with no obligation. We'll come out, measure your floor, assess its condition, and provide a detailed written quote. Most estimates for standard residential jobs can be scheduled within 1–2 business days. Call (954) 495-8331 or submit our online form to get started.

Can epoxy flooring handle Florida's humidity and moisture? +

Humidity and moisture vapor transmission (MVT) from the soil are the #1 cause of epoxy failures in Florida. We test every slab for moisture vapor before coating and use moisture-tolerant epoxy primers when needed. If your slab has high MVT readings, we recommend a polyaspartic or moisture-mitigating system specifically designed for South Florida conditions.

Epoxy Flooring Services in Miramar, FL

Complete coating solutions for garages, homes, and commercial spaces throughout Broward County.

All Epoxy Flooring Services

Every coating system is selected and installed to handle Florida's extreme heat, humidity, and UV exposure.

Garage Floor Epoxy

Our most popular residential service. We grind the concrete, patch cracks, apply a moisture-tolerant primer, and finish with a full broadcast flake or solid-color topcoat. Slip-resistant, oil-resistant, and built for Florida.

Typical cost: $1,100–$3,600 for 1–2 car garages

Metallic Epoxy Flooring

Create a swirling, three-dimensional effect with metallic pigments suspended in clear epoxy. Each floor is unique — perfect for showrooms, living areas, restaurants, and offices looking for a dramatic high-end look.

Typical cost: $5–$12 per sq ft

Full Details →

Commercial & Industrial Epoxy

Heavy-duty 100% solids epoxy systems for warehouses, auto shops, medical facilities, food processing plants, and retail floors. Chemical-resistant, forklift-rated, and designed for continuous heavy traffic.

Typical cost: Custom quote (volume pricing available)

Full Details →

Flake & Chip Broadcast Epoxy

Colored vinyl flakes are broadcast into wet epoxy to create a terrazzo-like finish that adds texture, grip, and visual depth. Available in dozens of color blends to match any home exterior or interior color scheme.

Typical cost: $3–$7 per sq ft

Full Details →

Polyaspartic Coatings

Fast-curing, UV-stable, and moisture-tolerant — polyaspartic is the ideal solution for Miramar's sunny, humid climate. Full-system jobs are completed in one day, and vehicles can return to the garage within hours.

Typical cost: $4–$8 per sq ft

Full Details →

Concrete Resurfacing

Severe spalling, scaling, or staining? We apply a cementitious overlay to level and restore damaged slabs before coating. Gives new life to concrete that would otherwise require a full replacement.

Typical cost: $3–$6 per sq ft

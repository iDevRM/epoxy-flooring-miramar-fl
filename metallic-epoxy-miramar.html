What Is Metallic Epoxy?

Metallic epoxy uses fine metallic pigment powders (aluminum, mica, or pearlescent) suspended in a clear epoxy resin. During application, the pigments flow and settle naturally — creating depth, movement, and a three-dimensional look that can't be replicated with paint or standard epoxy.

No two metallic epoxy floors are identical. The effect changes with light direction and angle, giving your floor a living, dynamic look. Popular effects include swirl patterns, lava flow, ocean wave, and marble-style veining.

Metallic Epoxy FAQ — Miramar, FL

How much does metallic epoxy flooring cost in Miramar? +

Metallic epoxy typically runs $5–$12 per square foot installed, depending on the complexity of the design, number of colors, and floor prep required. A 500 sq ft garage in metallic would run approximately $2,500–$6,000. We provide free on-site estimates — call (954) 495-8331.

Can metallic epoxy go over existing tile in Miramar homes? +

In many cases, yes — if the tile is firmly bonded, level, and free of adhesive voids. We assess each floor during the estimate. If the tile has loose sections or significant lippage, it's better to remove it first for the best result.

Will it fade or yellow in Florida's sun? +

Not if installed correctly. We always apply a UV-stable polyaspartic or aliphatic urethane topcoat over metallic epoxy — standard aromatic epoxy topcoats will yellow in South Florida's UV. Our systems are spec'd for Florida's sun exposure from the start.

Is metallic epoxy hard to maintain? +

It's actually very easy. A simple sweep and damp mop keeps metallic floors looking great. Avoid harsh chemicals — pH-neutral cleaners are best. The high-gloss topcoat means spills bead up and wipe off easily, and scratches or scuffs are easy to buff out.

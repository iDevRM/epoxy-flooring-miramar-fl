Garage Floor Epoxy Coating in Miramar, FL

Turn your plain concrete garage into a showroom-quality floor that handles Florida's heat, humidity, and oil drips without breaking a sweat.

Why Miramar Garages Need the Right Epoxy

South Florida garages face unique challenges: 90°F+ summer temps, year-round humidity, and concrete that often holds moisture vapor from the soil below. A DIY kit or improperly applied epoxy will peel, bubble, or delaminate within months under these conditions.

We use professional-grade 100% solids epoxy with a moisture-tolerant primer — not water-borne box-store products. Every job starts with diamond grinding to open the concrete pores for a mechanical bond that won't lift in the Florida heat.

Our Garage Floor Process

  1. Clear and mask the garage
  2. Diamond-grind the entire slab
  3. Repair cracks and control joints
  4. Apply moisture-tolerant epoxy primer
  5. Apply base coat (solid color or broadcast flake)
  6. Apply clear polyurethane or polyaspartic topcoat
  7. Final inspection and walkthrough

Garage Epoxy FAQ — Miramar, FL

How long before I can park my car on the new epoxy floor? +

Typically 48–72 hours for vehicle traffic. You can walk on the floor after 24 hours. In Miramar's summer heat, cure times can shorten slightly. We'll give you an exact timeline based on the products used and weather on your install day.

Will garage epoxy hold up to tire marks and hot tires? +

Our polyurethane and polyaspartic topcoats are specifically formulated to resist hot tire pickup — a common failure point with cheap water-borne epoxy systems. Tires sitting on hot Florida pavement and then driven onto a poorly coated floor will peel the surface over time. Our topcoats prevent this.

What colors and styles are available for garage epoxy? +

We offer solid colors, full-broadcast flake systems in dozens of color blends, and metallic options. Flake systems are the most popular for Miramar garages — they add texture, hide dust and debris, and look great with both light and dark finishes. We'll bring samples to your estimate.

Does the garage need to be completely empty? +

Yes — completely cleared floor to wall. Everything must be off the slab: cars, shelving units, gym equipment, stored boxes. This ensures even grinding and coating application. We'll give you advance notice so you have time to prepare.

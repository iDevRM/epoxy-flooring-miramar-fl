Commercial Epoxy FAQ — Miramar, FL

Can commercial epoxy be installed after hours to avoid downtime? +

Yes — we schedule commercial work around your operation hours, including evenings and weekends. Fast-cure polyaspartic systems allow re-occupancy within hours, minimizing disruption to your Miramar business.

How thick is commercial epoxy vs. residential? +

Residential garage systems are typically 10–15 mils total dry film thickness. Commercial systems are 20–60+ mils depending on traffic demands, with some heavy industrial systems exceeding 125 mils (an eighth of an inch). We specify system thickness based on your facility's load requirements.

What square footage do you typically handle for commercial projects? +

We regularly handle commercial projects from 1,000 sq ft to 50,000+ sq ft throughout Broward County. Large industrial projects are quoted with volume pricing. Call (954) 495-8331 to discuss your project scope.
